TapTap is a free mobile game where two players tap as fast as they can in a real-time match. This policy explains what we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean the operator of TapTap named in section 1.

The short version

  1. We collect a display name you choose, a random device ID, your match results and basic technical data. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
  2. Analytics and session replay are only used with your consent, and session replay is only for adults. Replays can show other players' names on screen. Text you type into fields is masked.

  6. Deleting your account ends your sessions at once and removes your personal identifiers within 30 days. Some match and coin records stay, anonymised, so other players' results and rewards stay correct.

5. Children and teenagers

Under 13: not allowed

TapTap is not for children under 13. When you start the app you enter your date of birth on a neutral age screen. If you are under 13, the screen says the game is not available to you. We do not create an account and do not collect analytics about you. See our children's page for more.

Teens aged 13 to 17

  • You see non-personalised ads only.
  • Session replay is off.
  • Usage analytics is off unless you turn it on.
  • In India and the USA, teens get the standard ad settings, unless the region requires consent. Where a US state requires consent (for example California), teens get the stricter settings. [LEGAL REVIEW]

Your date of birth

We use your date of birth once to work out your age group. We do not store it, log it or send it to analytics.

6. Your consent and how to withdraw it

Where we need your consent, you can change your mind at any time:

  • Analytics and session replay: Settings, then Privacy choices. Turn off "Analytics and improvement". This stops new data straight away and needs no restart.
  • Ad choices: Settings, then Privacy choices, then Ad privacy options. This opens Google's consent form.
  • Tracking on iPhone: Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking. Turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  • Ads on Android: Settings, then Google, then Ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation.

Withdrawing consent takes effect immediately for new processing. It does not undo what was already done lawfully before you withdrew.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings, then Delete account. You can also use our deletion page. Deletion works like this:

  • Immediately: your sessions end. The account can no longer be used to play, sign in or appear on boards.
  • Within 30 days: your personal identifiers are removed. This includes your display name, username, avatar and cosmetics, and the link to your device.
  • Analytics: your analytics profile is deleted from PostHog.
  • Kept, but anonymised: your match results and coin ledger entries. We keep them so that other players' results and rewards stay correct. They no longer identify you.
  • Reports: reports about a deleted account are kept anonymised for 90 days for abuse review, then deleted. Reports you sent are deleted.
  • Backups: copies in backups expire on each provider's schedule, [OWNER: confirm Supabase backup retention]. If we restore a backup, we repeat any deletions that were requested after it was made.

Inactive guest accounts: a guest account with no activity for 24 months goes through the same steps.
