PRIVACY POLICY — TAP & TAP

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Tap & Tap ("the game", "we", "us") is a free mobile game where two players tap as fast as they can in a real-time match. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the game you agree to this policy.

Contact: sonubodat77@gmail.com

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

a) Information you provide

  • Display name. You can pick a name (up to 20 characters). Other players, leaderboards and challenge links can show it. Please do not use your real name or any personal information.

b) Information created when you play

  • A random guest identifier. The game creates an anonymous guest account for you with a random device identifier stored on your phone. It is not your hardware ID, phone number, email or name.
  • Gameplay data: match results, taps, scores, win/loss and streak statistics, power-up inventory and rewards you earn, challenges you create or accept, and leaderboard rank.
  • Technical data: app version, operating system (Android or iOS), connection timing used to detect slow connections, and error and security logs.
  • Analytics events: simple events such as "app opened", "match finished" or "reward ad watched", linked to your anonymous player ID, used to understand how the game is used and to fix problems.

c) Information collected by advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and (depending on your device settings) personalize ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and the game does not use them.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • To run the game: create your guest account, match you with opponents, keep scores, rewards and leaderboards.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse and to keep the game fair and secure.
  • To measure and improve the game, and to fix bugs.
  • To show ads and give rewards for watching rewarded ads.
  • To respond to requests you send us.

3. OPPONENTS AND BOTS

If no other player is found quickly, the game may match you with a computer-controlled opponent. Bot opponents use a made-up name and are not real people. Bot matches work like normal matches.

4. WHAT OTHER PLAYERS CAN SEE

Your display name, rank and public match statistics can appear on leaderboards and in challenge links you share. We do not show your device identifier or any contact details.

5. SHARING OF INFORMATION

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers that host and run the game (hosting, database and caching providers).
  • Google AdMob, for advertising as described above.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of players and the game.

6. DATA RETENTION

Gameplay data is kept while your guest account is active. Logs and analytics events are kept for a limited time needed for security and improvement. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see Section 8).

7. CHILDREN

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it. Advertising shown in the game is general audience advertising.

8. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

  • You can change your display name in Settings.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking / Apple Advertising).
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the data linked to your guest account by emailing sonubodat77@gmail.com with your display name and, if possible, your player ID. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, or California), you may have additional rights such as objecting to or restricting processing. We will respond within a reasonable time.
  • Uninstalling the game removes the guest identifier from your phone; server-side data can be deleted on request.

9. SECURITY

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). Game results are validated on our servers. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Our service providers and Google may process data in countries other than yours. By using the game you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
