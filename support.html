SUPPORT — TAP & TAP

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Need help with Tap & Tap? You're in the right place. We want the game to be fair, fun and easy to use, and we are happy to help with questions about gameplay, progress, rewards, ads, account issues, and reportable problems.

Contact: sonubodat77@gmail.com

1. HOW TO CONTACT US

If you need help, please email us at sonubodat77@gmail.com. Please include as much detail as you can so we can review the issue properly.

  • Your device type and operating system
  • App version, if known
  • A short description of the problem
  • When the issue happened and whether it is repeatable
  • Your display name or player ID, if relevant

2. COMMON HELP TOPICS

Account and progress

The game uses a guest account tied to your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your progress and items may be lost. We are not responsible for lost progress caused by deleting the app, clearing storage, or reinstalling without a backup.

Gameplay and matchmaking

If a match feels stuck, delayed, or unfair, please tell us what happened. We review issues like connection problems, lag, disconnects, rewards not appearing, or bad match results.

Rewards, power-ups, and ads

Rewarded ads and power-ups are provided by the game and may be limited by cooldowns, server checks, or availability. If you believe you did not receive a reward after watching an ad, email us with the date and time of the event.

Cheating, abuse, and moderation

If you suspect another player is cheating, using modified apps, scripts, or other unfair methods, please report the issue to us with the match details and the suspected player name if available. We may investigate and take action such as resetting scores, removing unfair gains, or suspending accounts.

3. BUG REPORTS AND FEATURE FEEDBACK

We welcome feedback. If you see a bug, please tell us what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and what you were doing when it occurred. This helps us reproduce issues and improve the game.

4. RESPONSE TIME

We try to answer support requests as quickly as possible, but response times may vary depending on volume and the complexity of the issue. We appreciate your patience while we investigate.

5. PRIVACY REQUESTS

If you want to access, correct, delete, or review data related to your guest account, please email us at sonubodat77@gmail.com with your display name and any player ID or other relevant details. You can also read our Privacy Policy for more information.

6. FINAL NOTE

We may update the game, its rules, rewards, or support process over time. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the current version of the rules and policies.
