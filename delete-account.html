DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

TapTap · Developer: [OWNER: developer name as on Google Play] · Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can delete your TapTap account and its data in the app, or by sending a request on this page. You do not need to reinstall the app to use the web request. Deletion does not need you to contact us first.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open TapTap and go to Settings.
  2. Go to Legal.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the list of what is deleted and kept, and confirm.

The app then shows a "Deletion in progress" screen with a reference number.

2. Request deletion on the web

Use this form if you cannot open the app. Enter your player tag or username. If you have your deletion code (shown in Settings > Legal), enter it too: the code lets us match the request to your account without any further checks. Your contact email is optional and is used only to reply to this request.

Shown in Settings > Legal in the app. Copy it when you can, so you still have it if you lose the device.
Used only to reply to this request.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Identifiers: guest account ID, player tag, display name, username, avatar Deleted within 30 days.
Friends, friend requests, blocks, social settings, privacy consents Deleted within 30 days.
Analytics profile held for your player ID (product analytics service) Deleted within 30 days.
Match results and the coin ledger Kept, anonymised. Your name and identifiers are removed, so the records no longer point to you. They are kept so that your opponents' results and coin history remain correct.
Reports filed against your account Kept, anonymised for 90 days for abuse review, then deleted.

Anonymised match and coin records do not contain your name or identifiers after deletion.

4. Without the app or your deletion code

  • Inactivity: a guest account with no activity for 24 months is anonymised automatically, even if you never submit a request.
  • Manual verification: email sonubodat77@gmail.com with your player tag and the email address you used for the request. We may ask you for more details to confirm the account belongs to you before we delete it.

5. Timeline

  • Your request is recorded immediately. You get a reference number to keep.
  • Your account is disabled straight away: you can no longer play or sign in with that device account.
  • Deletion is completed in the background. It is expected to be finished within 30 days.
  • If you gave a contact email, we reply to it when the request is complete or if we need more information.
