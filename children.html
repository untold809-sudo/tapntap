Children

Version 2 · Effective 24 November 2026

TapTap is not for children under 13. This page explains how we keep younger children out, what we do if we find out a child under 13 used the game, and how a parent or guardian can contact us.

Under 13 is not allowed

When you first open TapTap, you enter your date of birth. The age screen does not suggest a default age and does not say what the cutoff is.

If you are under 13, the screen says the game is not available to you. We do not create an account for you, and we do not collect analytics about you.

Teens aged 13 to 17 can play, with limits on ads, session replay and analytics. See section 5 of our Privacy Policy.

If we learn a child under 13 used TapTap

If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account or used the game, we close that account straight away and delete its data. We follow the same steps as in section 7 of our Privacy Policy. Some anonymised match and coin records may remain so that other players' results and rewards stay correct.

Parents and guardians

If you think your child has used TapTap, email sonubodat77@gmail.com. Include the display name or username your child used, and the device they played on, if you know them. You do not need to send us your child's date of birth or any other personal details beyond that.
